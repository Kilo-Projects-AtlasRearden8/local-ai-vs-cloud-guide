# Running AI Locally vs. Cloud: A Beginner's Manual

A plain-English beginner's manual for choosing between running AI on your own
computer and using cloud AI services. Covers picking models, RAM and VRAM
basics, Ollama and LM Studio setup, privacy benefits, cost comparison, and
when to use each. **No API key and no account required** to get started.

Written and published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October
2026) — beginner-friendly AI education, books and guides, and practical tools.
Part of the family around Dylan Asdale's *The Massive AI Manual* and the hub
site dylanasdale.github.io, where AI is explained "like a friend would": plain
English, step by step, never condescending.

## Who this is for

You don't need to be a programmer. You don't need a credit card. You don't
need an account. Everything in this manual works on a regular laptop or
desktop.

This was written for beginners — small-business owners, authors, nurses,
teachers, retirees, students, and anyone who wants AI explained clearly and
kindly. If you've ever wondered whether you should run AI locally or in the
cloud, start here.

## Quick start

1. Read the manual: open `docs/00-read-me-first.md` or `site/index.html` in
   your browser.
2. Pick a path: local-first (Ollama or LM Studio) or cloud-first, or mix both.
3. Follow the setup steps — no credit card, no API key, no account needed for
   local AI.
