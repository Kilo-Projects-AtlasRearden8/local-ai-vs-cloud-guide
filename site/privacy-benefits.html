Privacy Benefits

One of the best reasons to run AI locally is privacy: your conversations, documents, and questions stay on your computer. This section explains what that means, what it doesn't mean, and how to stay safe.

What "local" means for privacy

When you run a model with Ollama or LM Studio:

  • Your text never leaves your computer to reach the model.
  • No company reads your chats to train their models.
  • No account is tied to your conversations.
  • No internet connection is needed after the model is downloaded.
  • You can delete a model or a chat anytime, and it's gone from your machine.

This makes local AI a good fit for personal journals, draft business letters, client notes, health-related questions (for education, not medical advice), and anything you wouldn't want posted online.

What local AI does NOT protect you from

Local AI is private, but it is not magic security:

  • Malware: only download models from trusted sources (Ollama's library, Hugging Face, LM Studio's built-in browser). Don't run random model files from strangers.
  • Your computer's security: if someone else can log into your computer, they can read your local chat history. Use a login password and lock your screen.
  • Backups: if you back up your computer to a cloud service, your chat history may be included in that backup. Check what your backup tool saves.
  • Outputs you choose to share: copying an AI answer into an email or website sends it wherever that email or website goes.

Cloud AI: the trade-off

Cloud AI services are convenient and often more powerful, but they usually:

  • Require an account and sometimes a credit card.
  • Send your text to the provider's servers.
  • May use your inputs to train future models (check their settings; many let you opt out).
  • Keep logs of your conversations for some period of time.

Cloud is fine for public information and casual use. For personal, client, or business-sensitive material, local-first is the safer default.

A simple privacy rule: if you wouldn't read it out loud in a public place, run it locally.
